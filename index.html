image-based 3D reconstruction vs LiDAR

How close does photogrammetry get to a laser scan?

Road authorities must inventory street furniture, and a laser scanner is not always at hand — so how accurately can photographs alone recover this geometry, against a LiDAR scan?

Four methods, two families: correspondence pipelines (COLMAP, hloc+COLMAP) and feed-forward models (MASt3R-GA, VGGT). They fail in opposite ways — a correspondence pipeline leaves a hole, a feed-forward model can invent geometry that is wrong.

Scored on a 1 cm grid by accuracy, completeness and F1 at 3/5/10 cm; scale from a 9.5 cm marker in frame, not from the reference.

Tool
DBSCAN gap tuner

Four of the six references are incomplete — parts were never scanned. A point there looks far from the reference, but that is missing reference, not error.

DBSCAN splits the two by shape: clustered far points mark a gap and are excluded, isolated ones are real noise and stay counted. The sliders show how much each conclusion depends on that line.
